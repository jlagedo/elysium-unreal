## Resolution

The 4a draft join (retail name in a comment) was wrong or incomplete on about 60 rows. Examples:
the Scurrying keys all pointed at one member, 20 existing carriers were read as "declare", and the
SabbatLeader `NPCInit` words carried no offset comment. Every row was re-joined by hand against the
declarations (evidence: `step4/cands2.txt`, `field_res.py`, `unclaimed.py`). Each absent row was
audited against the corpus accesses and `overrides-step3.tsv` (`absent_check.py`). Every accessor is
unported residue, a task arm, or one of two named seams: AndreiBlood `0x1035d010` and MingXiao
`0x10392a10`.

The findings recorded in `decisions-step4.json`:

- **Duplicate carriers:** 11 retail words have 2–3 port members.
- **Split carriers:** four port members stand for another class's word too.
- **Zombie gib latch:** `+0x66e0` is saved but never written, and retail's latch is `+0x66e1`.
- **Zombie AI type:** the `ZombieAIType` key is a plain write; `SetZombieAIType` runs only through
  the input or the maker.

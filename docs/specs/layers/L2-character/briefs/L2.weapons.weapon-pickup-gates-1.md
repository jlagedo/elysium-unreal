# Brief L2.weapons.weapon-pickup-gates-1 — Weapon use and pickup gates

Layer L2 · story `L2.weapons.weapon-pickup-gates` · slice 1 of 1 · **ready**

## Goal

These predicates jointly decide whether a weapon can be used or offered as a loose pickup. The story: Character `Weapon_CanUse` and weapon capability queries preserve readiness, ownership and inventory-insertion checks; loose pickup reports mask `0x49`, and ObjectCaps adds bit 8 only when the owner handle does not resolve (`0x1032d9b0`, `0x10257850`, `0x10257930`).

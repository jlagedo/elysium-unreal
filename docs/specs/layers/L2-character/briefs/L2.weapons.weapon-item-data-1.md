Layer L2 · story `L2.weapons.weapon-item-data` · slice 1 of 1 · **ready**

## Goal

Initialization, nested record loading and flag accumulation form one item-data load path. The story: Initialize weapon record defaults, load magazine and nested presentation data, and build the equipment mask from classname and authored flags (`0x10258b00`, `0x10258ef0`, `0x1025b630`).

## The retail functions (read each body before writing: `vtmb_code <address>`)

- `0x10258b00` Global::FUN_10258b00 (231 B, 61 maps, partial) — Initializes magazine and activation record slots, invalid lookup IDs, zero counters and unit-valued defaults. Port lacks: Native record construction resets represented fields, but the reduced schema lacks retail's two magazine banks and complete activation/effect slot state and sentinels.
- `0x10258ef0` Global::FUN_10258ef0 (436 B, 61 maps, partial) — Loads magazine records, ammunition labels/models/descriptions, reload timing, quantities, worth and nested data. Port lacks: Loads one magazine's type, capacity and timing. Missing additional magazines, ammo_worth, ammunition presentation fields and nested data; Dropped_Ammo defaults to zero instead of capacity.
- `0x1025b630` Global::FUN_1025b630 (200 B, 61 maps, partial) — Seeds an equipment mask from classname substrings, then lowercases and tokenizes authored flags and accumulates their bits. Port lacks: Token lookup and accumulation exist, but the accumulator starts at zero rather than classname-derived 0x50 or 1; retail's bounded input copy is also absent.

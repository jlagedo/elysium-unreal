## Goal

Implement reaction record and lookup helpers after their scope inputs are available. The story: Reproduce reaction record construction, loading and lookup at 0x101f0990, 0x101f0a60, 0x101f12e0, 0x101f14a0 and 0x101f1750. Preserve scope matching and retail candidate order.

## The retail functions (read each body before writing: `vtmb_code <address>`)

- `0x101f0990` Global::FUN_101f0990 (145 B, 108 maps, partial) — Identify one of five reaction modifier tokens and parse its integer operand, otherwise defaulting to addition. Port lacks: Signed addition and multiplication are parsed. Other retail operators and the bare-integer fallback are missing; numeric parsing uses doubles rather than atoi, and token matching differs.
- `0x101f0a60` Global::FUN_101f0a60 (81 B, 108 maps, partial) — Parse reaction Targets, read WhoModifies and decode Modifier into operator and amount. Port lacks: The fields and target parsing are present, but Modifier uses the incomplete parser. The port also makes WhoModifies a recognition gate, whereas this retail function only reads and discards it.
- `0x101f12e0` Global::FUN_101f12e0 (165 B, 108 maps, partial) — Open a reaction data file, parse each ReactionCategory and return failure on open or category-parse failure. Port lacks: Category/group traversal is folded into the modifier loader. Loading is fixed to reactions000.txt, malformed entries are skipped rather than propagating category failure, and modifier parsing is incom
- `0x101f14a0` Global::FUN_101f14a0 (530 B, 108 maps, partial) — Reset reactions, load General metadata and six modifier tokens/lengths, register tables and strings, and load reaction categories. Port lacks: Bands, labels, and category/group modifiers are loaded, but modifier tokens are hardcoded; General metadata, the full support-table/string registration, and sequential reaction-file traversal are miss
- `0x101f1750` Global::FUN_101f1750 (272 B, 108 maps, partial) — Populate existing reaction groups from sequential files and cache ReactionLevel, Reaction_Ranges, and Appearance_Reaction lookup IDs. Port lacks: Reaction modifiers and band labels have equivalents, but only reactions000.txt is read; Appearance_Reaction loading and the three cached registry lookups are absent.

Layer L2 · story `L2.weapons.weapon-selection` · slice 1 of 1 · **ready**

## Goal

Availability and candidate ranking feed the final active-weapon switch. The story: Switch the active weapon and select the best ranged or next carried weapon after switch, item-state, deployment and wield checks, preserving the melee/ranged availability predicates and retail weight threshold (`0x1032dde0`, `0x10336cd0`, `0x10336d70`, `0x10337080`, `0x101abd40`).

# Timed law activity channels

Layer L0 · `L0.rules_cvars.timed-law-channel` · size M · 2 rows, 514 bytes of retail code

**Goal.** At 0x1017df50, reset pl_supernatural_level to -1, clear level and deadline on zero, or raise without lowering while refreshing the deadline and act count. Its callee 0x1017deb0 returns a positive explicit duration, otherwise max(previous retained level, live pl_min_act_timer), whose retail default is 5 seconds.

**Order.** The setter calls the shared duration helper, so implement and settle the helper before the setter's cvar and state writes.

## Rows

- `0x1017deb0` Global::FUN_1017deb0 (115 B, 108 maps, partial) — Return a positive explicit duration; otherwise return the greater of the previous level and the duration cvar's float value, with commands treated as zero. Port lacks: The explicit-duration branch and max arithmetic are folded into WriteTimedChannel. The cvar lookup and command branch are replaced by an explicitly unrecovered, chosen 2-second constant.
- `0x1017df50` Global::FUN_1017df50 (399 B, 108 maps, partial) — Reset the supernatural override cvar to -1, clear level and deadline on zero, otherwise raise or refresh the level's deadline and increment its act count. Port lacks: Clear, raise, refresh and count behavior is implemented. Missing the override-cvar reset and live duration-cvar read; the floor is an explicitly chosen 2 seconds. The port also clamps requests to 0..5

## Records

- `l0_law_derived_duration` (arena): With pl_min_act_timer at its retail default, fire SetSupernaturalLevel and SetCriminalLevel on !player at t=0 with level 3, at t=1 with level 1, and at t=2 with zero. Probe after each action: nonzero writes retain level 3, refresh deadlines using max(previous level, cvar), and increment counts even for the lower request; zero clears level and deadline without incrementing. At t=3 set pl_min_act_timer to 7 and fire SetCriminalLevel(2); its deadline uses the live 7-second value, and the supernatural override remains -1. Needs: input, script, NEW: player_law probe

Probe immediately: retail stores level 8 without a low-level clamp, sets deadline to t+1.25, increments the count once, and resets the supernatural override cvar to -1. Needs: NEW: set_law_channel action, NEW: player_law probe

## 0x101b30d0 Global::FUN_101b30d0 (152 B)

- **Signature:** `undefined4 * __fastcall FUN_101b30d0(undefined4 *desc)`. `ECX = desc`; returns `desc` in `EAX`. Body `0x101b30d0`–`0x101b3167`. No branches. It is the constructor of the sound-parameters record (Source's `CSoundParametersInternal`, here with name buffers).
- **Callers (four, through the thunk `0x1000731f`, `JMP 0x101b30d0`):**

## Goal

Calculate threshold damage and contact force before constructing and applying the collision damage packet.. The story: Map energy thresholds at `0x10151b70`, calculate mass and velocity-change damage at `0x10151c00`, derive contact force at `0x101520a0`, and route the packet through `0x100a8400` or `0x100a1580`.

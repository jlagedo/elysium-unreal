## Goal

The story: At 0x100b2150, 0x100b1ac0, 0x100b2300, and 0x100b2510, retail resolves a parent or attachment transform, rebuilds dirty absolute state, writes the absolute frame, and derives local origin and angles through the parent's inverse transform.

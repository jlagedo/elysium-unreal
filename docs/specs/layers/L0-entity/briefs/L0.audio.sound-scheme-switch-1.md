## Goal

Track retirement and slot configuration are callees needed by manager switching.. The story: At 0x1022a300 and 0x1022b590, register a scheme and, when enabled, clamp its fade duration, guard repeated activation, disable other schemes and schedule thinking; 0x10229270, 0x10229430 and 0x10229550 prune handles, configure four tracks, reuse matching filenames and retire outgoing tracks in retail order.

## Goal

Activation depends on the completed manager transition and registers before applying start_enabled.. The story: At 0x1022a300 and 0x1022b590, register a scheme and, when enabled, clamp its fade duration, guard repeated activation, disable other schemes and schedule thinking; 0x10229270, 0x10229430 and 0x10229550 prune handles, configure four tracks, reuse matching filenames and retire outgoing tracks in retail order.

Port lacks: FadeIn reaches playback and avoids restarting the same active scheme. Missing retail duration clamping, per-entity enabled state, player scheme-handle write, and think scheduling.

Port lacks: Start-enabled activation is folded into Spawn and deferred playback. It requests an instant fade, lacks manager entity registration, and does not reproduce the Activate/think branches.

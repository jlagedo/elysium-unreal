# Brief L0.audio.ambient-init-1 — Ambient sound initialization

Layer L0 · story `L0.audio.ambient-init` · slice 1 of 3 · **ready**

## Goal

This is one keyvalue-to-spawn initialization sequence. The story: At 0x101ada80, 0x101ad0f0 and 0x101ac310, parse and clamp ambient modulation keys, seed preset and fixed-point pitch, fade, spin and LFO state, then initialize sound level, flags, source handle and think state before precaching.

## The retail functions (read each body before writing: `vtmb_code <address>`)

- `0x101ada80` CAmbientGeneric::vfunc110 (1110 B, 95 maps, partial) — Parse ambient modulation keyvalues, clamp their ranges, convert rates to fixed-point values and delegate unknown keys. Port lacks: Pitch and fade keys are consumed, but with different conversion/clamping. Preset, pitchstart, volstart, spinup/down, LFO and cspinup fields and their retail transforms are missing.
- `0x101ad0f0` Global::FUN_101ad0f0 (590 B, 95 maps, partial) — Initialize ambient volume, apply presets and seed fixed-point pitch, fade, spin and LFO modulation state. Port lacks: Basic health-derived volume and authored pitch/fades are read. Presets, initial-versus-running values, fixed-point accumulators, spin state, LFO state and retail's pitch-101 adjustment are absent.

Port lacks: Looping and no-SFX/no-voice-duck routing are carried. Retail sound-level conversion, skip-collide flag, source-handle initialization and exact activation/think sequencing are missing.

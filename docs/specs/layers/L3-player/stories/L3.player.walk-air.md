# Player walking and air movement

Layer L3 · `L3.player.walk-air` · size M · 4 rows, 3164 bytes of retail code

**Goal.** Reproduce the full walking driver, planar air acceleration and sliding, grounded wish velocity, base velocity, and the gravity-free movement variant at 0x10121ce0, 0x10121120, 0x101213b0, and 0x10122c30.

**Order.** The walking driver selects ground, air, water, and jump branches and calls the lower movement helpers.

## Rows

- `0x10121ce0` CGameMovement::vfunc12 (927 B, 108 maps, partial) — Full walking-movement driver: sequences gravity, jumping, friction, walking/swimming, ground checks, falling and eye-offset transitions. Port lacks: Core walking, gravity and landing paths exist. Missing water-jump handling, base-velocity bookkeeping and animation-state-dependent eye transitions; water detection is unwired.
- `0x10121120` CGameMovement::vfunc8 (299 B, 108 maps, partial) — Build a normalized planar wish direction/speed, air-accelerate, add player base velocity and perform sliding movement. Port lacks: Planar wish calculation, air acceleration and sliding are carried. AirMove does not add the player's m_vecBaseVelocity before the movement trace.
- `0x101213b0` CGameMovement::vfunc11 (1819 B, 108 maps, partial) — Computes grounded wish velocity, accelerates or substitutes authored movement, adds base velocity and selects between flat and stepped collision paths. Port lacks: Wish acceleration, authored substitution and step-distance selection exist. Missing base velocity, retail low-speed cutoff, per-command wish/step outputs and exact trace/step gates.
- `0x10122c30` CGameMovement::vfunc18 (119 B, 108 maps, open) — Runs a movement variant without gravity: refreshes water, handles the jump latch and moves with base velocity temporarily added. Port lacks: No corresponding movement-mode wrapper or dispatch exists. Shared jump and collision helpers do not implement its base-velocity sequence.

**Goal.** Reproduce scene time at 0x1007dfa0, case-insensitive controller lookup and zero-on-miss at 0x100b5d10, and combo-side flex sampling at 0x10074610.

**Order.** These helpers provide scene time, controller selection, and the sampled flex value consumed by NPC AddFlexAnimation.

## Rows

Port lacks: ScenePlayer exposes a real current-time getter, but the NPC-facing SceneTimeOf returns zero and explicitly does not reach that clock. Gesture wind-back therefore lacks the retail input.

Port lacks: The sole caller, AddFlexAnimation, is empty. No corresponding track sampler or combo-side scaling was found. Retail assembly supplies floating-point calculations omitted by the packet's decompile.

**Goal.** At `0x103380d0`, obtain the character-template index through `GetCharTemplate` (`0x10337860`), resolve it through the template manager (`0x101d5e80`), and return the byte at template offset `+0x90`.

**Order.** Independent of the relationship and squad rows; its character-template and manager lookups are already ported lower-layer services.

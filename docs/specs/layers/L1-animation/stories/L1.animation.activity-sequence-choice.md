# Choose activity sequences with retail draws

Layer L1 · `L1.animation.activity-sequence-choice` · size L · 6 rows, 1053 bytes of retail code

**Goal.** At 0x10427df0, collect ordered candidates and weights with the retail flag exclusions, descriptor limit, and include-shadowing rule. At 0x10428060 and 0x1008de20, reuse the stored draw for same-sequence choice; the three stat entry points at 0x1008f3c0, 0x1008f480, and 0x1008f600 delegate with stat -1.

**Order.** The public selectors depend on the model's candidate list and stored draw; their wrappers follow the selector primitives.

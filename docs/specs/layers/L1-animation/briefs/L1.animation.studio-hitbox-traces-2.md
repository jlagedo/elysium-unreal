## Goal

Skeleton evaluation feeds bone setup, which populates the gameplay cache.. The story: At 0x10095fe0, calculate sequence pose, pose adjustments, and controllers; 0x10092b50 builds requested-mask bone matrices and 0x10095460 caches them. The 0x100c4a40, 0x100c4d50, 0x100c5020, and 0x10240cb0 helpers perform studio hitbox sweeps and clips; 0x10095a20 and 0x100956e0 apply hitbox and collision results, with 0x10096b50 and 0x10097000 mapping active-set hitboxes to bones.

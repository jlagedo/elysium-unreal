# Brief L1.animation.sequence-motion-1 — Derive movement from sequence motion

Layer L1 · story `L1.animation.sequence-motion` · slice 1 of 1 · waits for 0x100ad1a0

## Goal

The ground-speed vector consumes sequence yaw, while the velocity slot adds the physics and airborne branches. The story: At 0x1008fab0, derive movement yaw from sequence linear motion; at 0x10094870, rotate that yaw by entity facing and scale by retail ground speed. At 0x10095e50, return base velocity for physics or airborne bodies and sequence-derived linear and angular velocity for grounded animation.
